Privacy

Kubb: a cottage weekend, at kubbgame.ca. Last updated October 18, 2026.

Kubb is a small game made by one person, Josh Litwin, in Canada. It doesn't show ads, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing.

Playing without an account

Your progress is kept in your own browser (its local storage). It stays on your device; clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It backs your save up so you can play on another device. When you sign in, we keep:

  • your email address, and the name and picture Google gives us if you sign in with Google,
  • a copy of your game save (weekends, wins, stickers and settings), and when it was last backed up,
  • which kind of browser last backed it up, so you can tell two saves apart.

Accounts and saves are stored with Supabase, in North America. Sign-in emails are sent by our email provider.

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings, Account, Delete my account. That deletes your account and its cloud save straight away. The copy in your browser stays until you clear it.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications for online play (Multiplayer), we keep your phone's push address (a web address your browser gives us for sending to it) with your account, and, while a match is on your screen, which match it is, so you aren't notified about a move you're watching. Notifications only say whose turn it is, that a friend joined, or that they want a rematch, with your friend's player name. They're sent through your browser's own push service (Apple's, Google's or Mozilla's). Turning them off in Multiplayer deletes the address straight away; turning them off in your phone's settings deletes it the next time we try to send one. Deleting your account deletes it too.

How people use the game

The game counts things like weekends started, matches won and lost, and which powers get picked, using PostHog. These counts are anonymous: they aren't tied to your name, email or account. No cookies are set for this, and nothing is recorded of your screen or your clicks.

Notes you send

If you send a note from the game (Settings, Send us a note), it goes to a Google Form with what you wrote and a few details to help fix bugs: the game's version, the screen you were on, your screen size and browser. Don't put anything private in a note.

Questions

Send a note from the game's Settings and Josh will get back to you if you include a way to reach you.
