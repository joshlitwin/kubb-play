Come up to the cottage.

KUBB

Bring the Old King home.

Throw wooden batons at the other side's kubbs, then knock over the king. Win your way through a weekend of lawn tournaments, and pick a new power after every win.

Play free
On your phone or computer. Nothing to install.

Pull back and let go

  • Pull back with one thumb to aim.
  • The circle shows where your baton can land.
  • The grass decides the rest.

The ruleField kubbs first, then the baseline. The king goes last. Hit him early and you lose.

Every throw counts

  • Wind gusts. The lawn has a slope.
  • Morning dew makes the grass slick until it dries.
  • On Sunday, the grounds crew runs the sprinkler.
  • Every rival shows their kit and their habit before you play. Learn it, and beat it.

Powers at work

33 powers in five kinds. You start with a few and pick up more as you win.

  • Guards sit on your kubbs and look after them by themselves.
  • Tricks change one baton for one throw. Heavy, Boomerang, Curve, Firecracker.
  • Skills are always with you, nothing to tap.
  • Picnic treats are one use each, then gone. Lemonade, butter tarts, a sun hat.
  • Specials build as you knock kubbs over, then you let them go.

Every win grows your bag

  • The pick: win a match, pick one of three powers.
  • The shops: spend your loonies at Hazel's stand and Norm's store.
  • The dock: train a power up a level with Grandpa.
  • Take a bet if you dare. Lose it and you're going home.

Climb the Lawn Ladder

Three days, three brackets, twelve matches. Win, and your marker walks up the ladder to the final. Each day the wind picks up, the rivals bring more powers, and someone good waits at the top.

  • Friday, the Family TrialsBeat the family on the cottage lawn. Cousin Vic waits in the final.
  • Saturday, the Brass MinnowThe lake's best at Norm's marina, in the wind. Lakeside Laurie in the final.
  • Sunday, the CountyThe fair, the grandstand, and Pearl Lachance.

The County final

Best of three against Pearl Lachance on the centre pitch. She's won the County five years running, and her notebook has an answer for your two strongest powers. Win, and the grandstand is on its feet.

She's had the Old King on her mantel since 1992.

Come back for another weekend

Win or lose, some things stay at the cottage.

  • Grandpa's door: ten rungs of clothes to earn.
  • Nana's jar: spare loonies buy a wind chime, Muskoka chairs, a canoe.
  • 70 stickers to find, some of them secret.
  • Heirlooms that change the rules, each with a cost.
  • New starter bags to pack.
  • Chill, Classic or Cutthroat. Cutthroat opens up later.
  • Pass and play: two of you, one phone.
